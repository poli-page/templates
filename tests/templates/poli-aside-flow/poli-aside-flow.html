Legal Glossary

Agreement

A legally binding contract between two or more parties that creates mutual obligations enforceable by law.

API (Application Programming Interface)

A set of protocols and tools that allows different software applications to communicate with each other.

Breach

A violation or infringement of a law, obligation, or agreement. A material breach may give rise to termination rights.

Confidential Information

Non-public information disclosed by one party to another under terms of confidentiality, including trade secrets, business plans, and technical data.

Data Controller

The entity that determines the purposes and means of processing personal data under GDPR and similar regulations.

Data Processor

An entity that processes personal data on behalf of a data controller, subject to the controller's instructions.

Force Majeure

Unforeseeable circumstances that prevent someone from fulfilling a contract, such as natural disasters, war, or government actions.

Indemnification

A contractual obligation of one party to compensate the other for losses or damages arising from specified circumstances.

Intellectual Property

Creations of the mind protected by law, including patents, copyrights, trademarks, and trade secrets.

Jurisdiction

The official power to make legal decisions and judgments, typically defined by geographic territory or subject matter.

Liability

Legal responsibility for one's acts or omissions. Limitation of liability clauses cap the amount of damages recoverable.

Mediation

A form of alternative dispute resolution where a neutral third party helps the disputing parties reach a mutually acceptable agreement.

SLA (Service Level Agreement)

A commitment between a service provider and a client that defines the expected level of service, including uptime guarantees and response times.

Termination

The ending of a contract, either by expiration, mutual agreement, or breach. May trigger specific obligations regarding data retention and transition.

Warranty

A promise or guarantee regarding the condition, quality, or performance of a product or service. Warranties may be express or implied.
