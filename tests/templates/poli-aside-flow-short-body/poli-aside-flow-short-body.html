Executive Summary

This quarter saw significant progress across all engineering workstreams. The platform migration to the new microservices architecture is 78% complete, ahead of schedule. Key milestones include the successful deployment of the document rendering engine and the completion of load testing for the batch processing pipeline.

Two critical risks have been identified: the dependency on a third-party authentication provider undergoing acquisition, and the potential impact of new EU regulations on data residency requirements. Mitigation plans are in place for both.
